## Finding: Trauma "Stage 1 / 손상" tier is mechanically inert

**File/Function:** `Assets/Scripts/BodyRules.cs:26` (`BodyState.Stage`) and `BodyBuild` formulas at `BodyRules.cs:52-56`; displayed in `Assets/Scripts/CampaignHUD.cs:108-111` (`Body()`).

**Issue:** The README documents the trauma progression as three meaningful waypoints: "외상 14 손상 → 30 자동 분노 적응 → 55 심각한 부상." The HUD reinforces this by giving Stage 1 ("손상", 14–29 trauma) its own label, color (gold tint), and a filling progress bar — presented as a distinct combat state, same as Stage 2/3.

However, every gameplay formula that reads body state (`HeavyDamage`, `RecoveryMultiplier`, `MoveMultiplier`, `DashSpeed`, `IncomingMultiplier`, `TattoosEnabled`) branches only on `Stage == 2` or `Stage == 3`. Stage 1 has no distinct branch anywhere — it behaves identically to Stage 0 in every combat/economy calculation. The only visible change at 14 trauma is a label swap and bar fill; no recovery penalty, no incoming-damage change, no move/dash change, no tattoo effect.

**Repro:** Take exactly 14–29 trauma to one limb and observe combat — attack recovery, movement speed, dash speed, incoming damage multiplier, and tattoo activation are all unchanged from the untouched state, while the HUD displays "손상" as if something shifted.

**Severity:** Low–medium. Not a crash or progression blocker, but it's a real mismatch between the documented/UI-communicated injury model and actual mechanics — players will reasonably expect some change at the first named threshold and get none, which undercuts the "몸을 걸고 싸우는 긴장감" design goal called out in the README's own MDA section.
