## Summary

I integrated the already-substantial `CampaignDirector`/`EconomyDomain`/`DungeonGeneration`/`CampaignHUD` (built by Codex) into a playable campaign, touching only my owned files:

**Game.cs**
- `Awake()` branches on `sceneRole`: Legacy keeps starter tattoos + `GameHUD` + `Phase.Camp`; other roles add `CampaignHUD` and `CampaignDirector` (calling `Initialize()` after player/camera setup), skipping legacy tattoos.
- `Update()` gates Enter/R run-restart and Esc pause-toggle and wave/`SpawnWave` auto-progression behind `sceneRole == Legacy`; choice keys (1/2) and hitstop resume logic stay global.
- `Choose()` now calls `CampaignDirector.I?.Action("adapt")`.
- `Start()` redirects to the `AshenThreshold` scene via `SceneManager.LoadScene` before running the smoke-test coroutine if the current scene isn't Legacy, preserving `--smoke-test`.

**Fighters.cs**
- `Barbarian`: added `CanMove` (Combat or Village) vs `CanAct` (Combat only), so village locomotion works but attack/dash/parry stay gated to combat (training included, since it sets `Phase.Combat`).
- `Impact()` applies `CampaignSession.WeaponMultiplier` only outside Legacy, and fires `Action("heavy-hit")` on a successful heavy hit.
- `TakeHit()`: dodge/parry now fire `Action("dodge")`/`Action("parry")`; added a `training` floor (hp never drops to 0) and skips trauma/adaptation entirely for incidental (non-`ignoreDefense`) training damage, while `ignoreDefense` forced hits (debug tools, tutorial) always bypass armor's `TraumaMultiplier` so threshold teaching is exact.
- `Enemy`: elites move at .55× speed, hit 1.5× harder, resist light stagger like bosses, take less knockback, render 1.3× scale with a gold eye tint, and `TakeHit` now calls `CampaignDirector.I?.EnemyDefeated(this)` on death.

**NordicArt.cs** — `World()` now reads `Game.I.sceneRole`: Forest/Mine get new distinct procedural dark environments (`ForestFloor`/`MineFloor`) with optional future plate override; Village tries `Art/Village-v1` (falls back to the old fjord look until the plate is copied in); Legacy/Lobby/Sanctum keep the old `AshenThreshold-v1` painted backdrop.

**PrototypeBuilder.cs** — added `CreateCampaign()` (menu **"Valhalla/Create campaign"**, i.e. `createcampaign`) building Lobby/MainVillage/DungeonForest/DungeonMine/DungeonSanctum + legacy AshenThreshold, each with the matching `Game.sceneRole`, with Lobby first in build settings. `Build()` now runs `CreateCampaign()` and builds all six scenes. The original `CreateScene()` menu is preserved for legacy-only scene creation.
