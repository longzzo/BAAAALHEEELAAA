**2. The per-part "기억" (memory) counter shown in the shop header is dead/misleading for 5 of 7 body parts.**
`CampaignHUD.cs:229` shows `기억 {Profile.Scars(tattooPart)}회` on every tab, implying (as it correctly does for RightArm/Legs, where `armScars`/`legScars` gate `echo`/`frost`) that this number unlocks the locked tattoo on that same tab. But `TattooCatalog.IsUnlocked` (`TattooProgression.cs:124-136`) never reads `headScars`, `chestScars`, `backScars`, `leftArmScars`, or `leftLegScars` — the actual gates for `pain_echo`/`ember`/`valor`/`giant_counter` are `survivorMarks`/`fireScars`/`valorMarks`/`giantScars`, shown only once in an unrelated footer line (`CampaignHUD.cs:246`) regardless of selected tab. `CampaignSession.Settle` (`DungeonGeneration.cs:153-155`) does increment and report these part-specific counters after every run, so a player will watch "머리의 기억" climb across runs and reasonably expect it to unlock something on the Head tab — it never will. This is a real functional contradiction baked into domain code, not just copy, and it directly undermines trust in the shop's own displayed logic on first (and later) visits.
